A NOTE FROM THE QUESTIONABLE DEPARTMENT

How to survive.

Choose the 1,000-Level Campaign to follow the original corridor, or open Question Modes for randomized aptitude, reasoning, verbal ability, and general knowledge sessions.

  • Correct answers earn points once per level. Replays are free, but do not farm points.
  • A hint costs 25 points once per level. Skipping costs 50 points once per level and unlocks the next room.
  • Wrong answers do not reduce your score. Retries are unlimited.
  • Practice mode supports Quick Play, Challenge, Endless, Daily, Mixed, and Custom sessions.
  • Your progress and sound preference stay in this browser using local storage; clearing it erases local saves.

Random sessions use browser randomness and avoid recently seen questions where possible. Different browsers often get different sequences, but a static site cannot coordinate globally unique questions. Daily sets use your device's local date, so time zones can differ.

This is fictional entertainment, not a real CAPTCHA or security product. It never uses your camera, microphone, location, or network to inspect you.
